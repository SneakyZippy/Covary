🚀 Release v1.2.8

This release focuses on granular behavioral analytics, improved onboarding, and significant performance optimizations for rapid data entry.

### 🌟 Key Improvements
- **Enhanced Circadian Analytics**: Granular hourly tracking for steps and app usage data.
- **Sleep Insights**: Automatic extraction of sleep start, wake-up times, and sleep midpoint from health data.
- **Improved Onboarding**: New "Research Presets" to quickly configure tracking priorities.
- **Startup Optimization**: Significantly reduced app launch latency for faster event logging.
- **Data Management**: Advanced filtering for raw event records and improved import/export compatibility.
- **Compliance Tracking**: Refined visualization of check-in compliance and window-based logging.
