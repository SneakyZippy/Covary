🎉 First Public Beta!

Welcome to Covary — a research tool for tracking behavioral patterns.

- Guided onboarding with research presets (Essential, Circadian, Health, Productivity, All-Inclusive)
- Morning/Afternoon/Evening check-in windows with smart notifications
- Quick-track buttons for frequent metrics on the home screen
- Today's Timeline and tracking suggestions
- Metric Insights with dual-metric comparison and circadian rhythm visualization
- Correlation Matrix and Lagged Trend analysis (1-7 day offsets)
- Health Connect integration (steps, sleep) with hourly granularity
- App Usage tracking with dynamic category management (Android)
- Full JSON data export/import with UUID-based identity
- Automatic update notifications via GitHub releases
- Customizable themes (7 accent colors) and dark mode
- Data Quality dashboard with compliance tracking
- Retrospective editing and raw data management
